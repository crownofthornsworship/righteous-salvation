# Righteous Salvation — Strategy Roadmap

## Product goal

Turn the six-commander solo playtest into a readable, balanced strategy game with distinct decks, smarter opponents, a guided campaign, and excellent mobile card interaction.

## Phase 1 — Rules and clarity

- Create an in-game rules reference with turn phases, keywords, targeting, combat, victory, and deck identity.
- Add card zoom, legal-target highlighting, cost preview, undo-before-confirm, and clearer Journey Log entries.
- Improve mobile hand spacing, drag/tap parity, battlefield scaling, and safe-area handling.
- Add a guided first match that teaches through real decisions.

## Phase 2 — Balance and deck identity

- Instrument matches for opening-hand quality, Faith curve, dead cards, game length, and win rate.
- Give David, Esther, Moses, Paul, Noah, and Ruth distinct strengths, weaknesses, and signature mechanics.
- Review all 240 cards for templating consistency, unintended loops, and meaningful singleton choices.
- Tune redraws and Blessing guarantees without removing deck-building tension.

## Phase 3 — Better opponents

- Replace generic move scoring with commander-aware priorities and multi-action planning.
- Add Easy, Standard, and Wise AI modes that differ in planning depth, not hidden bonuses.
- Teach the AI to hold answers, value board position, protect win conditions, and recognize lethal turns.

## Phase 4 — Campaign and modes

- Add a six-chapter campaign with scripted introductions, themed opponents, challenges, and unlocks.
- Add puzzle battles, daily seeded matches, commander mastery, and local match history.
- Add a final boss-style encounter that changes rules in clear, learnable phases.

## Phase 5 — Art and release quality

- Review biblical-era illustrations for consistency and historical details.
- Improve card frames, battlefield effects, commander reactions, attack resolution, and victory presentation.
- Preserve fast loading through atlases and responsive image formats.

## Release gate

- Run matchup and regression matrices for all commanders, redraw states, keywords, AI levels, and save migration.
- Test small-phone portrait/landscape, tablet, and desktop with touch, mouse, and keyboard accessibility.
- Refresh social preview, metadata, README, Light & Life Games, and Salt & Light Web Studio.
